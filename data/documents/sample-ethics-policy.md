# Sample Ethics Policy

This is a sample document so the app has something to answer questions about on
first run. Replace it with your own documents (PDF, TXT, or Markdown).

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any personal, financial, or family interest that could
influence their professional judgment. Disclosures are made to your manager and
recorded by the Ethics Office within five business days.

## Gifts and Hospitality

Employees may accept gifts of nominal value (under 50 USD). Gifts above this
threshold must be declined or, where refusal would cause offense, surrendered to
the Ethics Office. Cash and cash-equivalent gifts may never be accepted.

## Confidential Information

Confidential information must only be used for legitimate business purposes and
never disclosed to third parties without authorization. This obligation
continues after employment ends.

## Reporting Concerns

Concerns about unethical conduct can be raised anonymously through the Ethics
Hotline. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is
strictly prohibited.
